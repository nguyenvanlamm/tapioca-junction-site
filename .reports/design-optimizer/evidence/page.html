The track is the puzzle

A flipper sends one pearl its way, then flips by itself. Six pearls down one junction go left, right, left, right. You decide where the flippers point before you pour, then watch it run.

Three of a flavour, sealed

A cup takes three pearls of its flavour, then the press comes down. Pshh. The next cup slides up.

Misses come back

A pearl that reaches the wrong cup rides the straw up to the return jar. Tap it to send them again, oldest first.

Jammed, never timed

Fill the return jar and the counter jams. There is no clock and no lives: restart the order whenever you like.

How it plays

One order takes a minute or two. Thinking takes as long as you want.

  1. Set the routeTap a flipper to turn it. Pinned ones stay put.
  2. Pour a jarTap a jar. Its pearls drop one by one, bottom first, and every flipper they pass flips behind them.
  3. Seal every cupWhen the last cup is sealed, the bell rings: Order up!

A stamp for every flavour

Taro has a ring, matcha a leaf, mango a diamond, strawberry three dots, blueberry a star. You can tell pearls apart without relying on colour.

Three helps

Undo your last pour, Peek at a jar's whole route before you pour it, or open a Wide Jar for more return room. You start with a few and earn more the first time you clear milestone orders.

Quiet by design

No ads, no accounts, no notifications nagging you back. Sound effects you can switch off in Settings.

Questions

When can I play?
Tapioca Junction is coming soon to iPhone. This page will link to the App Store as soon as it is live.
Is there a timer or an energy system?
No. There are no timers, hearts or waits. A jam only ends that attempt, and Restart is always free.
Does it show ads or track me?
No ads, no analytics, no account. Progress stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
I'm colour-blind. Can I play?
Yes. Every flavour carries its own stamp inside the pearl and on its cup, so shape alone tells you where a pearl belongs.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything runs on the device; only purchases talk to the App Store.
