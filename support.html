How to play

  1. Tap a flipper to turn it. Every flipper sends a pearl the way it points, then flips by itself.
  2. Tap a jar to pour it: its pearls drop one by one through the tubes, bottom first.
  3. A cup takes three pearls of its flavour and is sealed. Seal every cup to finish the order.
  4. A pearl that misses goes up the straw to the return jar; tap the return jar to send it again, oldest first. If the return jar overflows, the counter jams: use Undo or Wide Jar, or restart for free.

Purchases

  • House Classics and Fruit Teas are free.
  • The Full Menu (a one-time purchase) opens Cheese Foam and Late Night: Cheese Foam order 1 is playable right after you buy it, and Late Night opens when you finish Cheese Foam.
  • The Barista's Apron adds 5 Undo, 5 Peek and 3 Wide Jar.
  • The Tip Cup is a thank-you and unlocks nothing.

Restore a purchase

Bought the Full Menu before, or on another device with the same Apple ID? Open Settings → Restore purchases (it is also in The Counter).

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
