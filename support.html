How to play

Tap a jar to pour it: its pearls drop one by one through the tubes. Every flipper sends a pearl the way it points, then flips by itself; tap a flipper first to turn it. A cup takes three pearls of its flavour and is sealed. A pearl that misses goes up the straw to the return jar; tap the return jar to send it again, oldest first. Seal every cup to finish the order. If the return jar overflows, the counter jams: use Undo or Wide Jar, or restart for free.

Purchases

House Classics and Fruit Teas are free. The Full Menu (a one-time purchase) opens Cheese Foam and Late Night: Cheese Foam order 1 is playable right after you buy it, and Late Night opens when you finish Cheese Foam. The Barista's Apron adds 5 Undo, 5 Peek and 3 Wide Jar. The Tip Cup is a thank-you and unlocks nothing. Bought the Full Menu before, or on another device with the same Apple ID? Open Settings → Restore purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
